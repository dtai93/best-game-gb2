# Ashwell — Lantern of the Last Well

A twin-stick defense roguelite. You guard a glowing well in a round stone arena for eight nights. Shades — moths, hounds, wisps, brutes, and hexers — cross the stones to snuff the lantern. Between nights the well offers three gifts and you take one. Night 8 is the boss, The Unlit Crown. Hold the oil until dawn to win. If the oil runs out, the well goes dark.

## Controls

- WASD to move
- Mouse to aim, click to fire
- Shift, right-click, or Space to dash
- F or Q for the halo pulse when the meter is full
- 1, 2, 3, or a click to take a gift between nights
- Esc to pause. Enter or Esc resumes
- M to mute
- R to return to the title
- Enter, Space, or click to begin, and to restart after dawn or dark

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. The game is static files under `game/` and does not use the network.
